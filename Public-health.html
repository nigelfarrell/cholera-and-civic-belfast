Overview

This section explores the development of public health infrastructure in nineteenth-century Belfast, including water supply, sanitation systems, and the emergence of civic health governance.

It is currently under development and will expand alongside ongoing research into cholera epidemics and urban reform.

Planned Themes

Water Supply: Belfast’s evolving water infrastructure and access inequality.

Sanitation: drainage, waste removal, and urban contamination.

Civic Reform: institutional responses to epidemic disease and urban growth.
