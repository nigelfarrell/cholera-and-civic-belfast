Belfast’s nineteenth century was shaped by rapid industrial growth, repeated epidemics, and major struggles over water, sanitation, and public health.

This site explores cholera epidemics, civic reform, and everyday life in nineteenth-century Belfast through archival research and historical narrative.

About the Project

Cholera & Civic Belfast is a public history project based on doctoral research into nineteenth-century Belfast. It explores the relationship between epidemic disease, urban development, and civic governance.

The site is an evolving scholarly resource and will continue to expand as research is developed and published.

About the Author

Dr Nigel Farrell is a historian specialising in cholera, public health and nineteenth-century Belfast. His research combines archival investigation with public history approaches to make historical material accessible to wider audiences.
