Research and writing by Dr Nigel Farrell
Home Epidemics Public Health People Places Blog Sources Research

Belfast’s nineteenth century was shaped by rapid industrial expansion, repeated epidemics, and intense struggles over water, sanitation, and civic reform.

This site explores cholera outbreaks, public health responses, and the lived experiences of those who navigated the transformation of the city into an industrial capital.
