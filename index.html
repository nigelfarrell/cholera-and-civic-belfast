Belfast’s nineteenth century was shaped by rapid industrial growth, repeated epidemics, and major struggles over water, sanitation, and public health. These pressures transformed the city’s physical environment and reshaped the ways in which its inhabitants lived, worked, and responded to crisis.

Through archival research and historical storytelling, Cholera & Civic Belfast explores cholera epidemics, civic reform, and everyday life in nineteenth-century Belfast.
