Stories of Disease, Public Health and Everyday Life in Nineteenth-Century Belfast
Research and writing by Dr Nigel Farrell
Home Epidemics Public Health People Places Sources Blog

Belfast’s nineteenth century was shaped by rapid industrial growth, repeated epidemics, and major struggles over water, sanitation, and public health. This website explores the people, places, and policies that shaped the city’s response to disease and its journey towards modern urban infrastructure.

Through archival research and historical storytelling, Cholera & Civic Belfast brings together cholera epidemics, civic reform, and everyday life in nineteenth-century Belfast.
