About this Page

This page is intended as a growing resource for archival collections, online repositories, recommended websites, and other research materials related to the history of cholera, public health, and nineteenth-century Belfast.

📚 Planned Content

In the near future, this page will include:

  • Recommended websites — Digital archives, historical societies, and research portals
  • Archive resources — Key archival collections for studying Belfast's public health history
  • Secondary sources — Selected scholarly works on cholera, urban history, and medicine in Ireland
  • Online repositories — Links to digitised newspapers, maps, and historical documents

For a full bibliography, please refer to the PhD Thesis.
