Global Cholera Pandemics 1817–1866

First Pandemic (1817–1824)

Origin: Bengal (Ganges Delta)
Reach: Asia, East Africa

Transmission routes: Trade routes across India, Southeast Asia, Middle East.

Key developments: First global expansion beyond South Asia.

No direct Belfast epidemic recorded in this phase.

Second Pandemic (1829–1837)

Origin: India → Russia → Europe
Reach: Europe, UK, Ireland, Americas

Key developments: Arrival in Europe (1831–32).

Includes: 1832 Belfast epidemic

Third Pandemic (1846–1860)

Origin: Bengal → global maritime spread
Reach: Worldwide

Key developments: Peak global mortality; John Snow's investigations.

Includes: 1848-49 Belfast epidemic and 1854 Belfast epidemic

Fourth Pandemic (1863–1875)

Origin: Bengal
Reach: Global

Key developments: Steamship expansion accelerates spread.

Includes: 1866 Belfast epidemic
